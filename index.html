An iPhone app that records a pull from your OBD-II adapter, a GPS and a GoPro at the same time, then saves the combined files the Virtual Dyno reads. No account, no cloud, nothing leaves your phone unless you share it.

get_the_app

Coming soon on theApp Store

Free for iPhone, iOS 17 or later.
Bluetooth needs a real iPhone, so there is no iPad or Mac version.

how_it_works

  1. Pair your adapter, GPS and GoPro on the Devices tab. They reconnect on their own after that.
  2. Type the vehicle name and press record.
  3. Floor it in one gear from low RPM to redline on a flat, straight road, then stop.
  4. Open the Virtual Dyno and load the .csv and .gpx.

Tip: a coast in neutral from about 70 to 30 MPH in the same run lets the dyno fit rolling resistance.

The CSV uses Cobb Accessport column names and the GPX carries speed on every point, so the dyno reads both without any settings.

privacy

No tracking. No account, no ads, no analytics. Your runs stay on your iPhone until you share them. From version 1.1 the app sends one thing: your location rounded to about 1 km, to Open-Meteo, to fetch the weather for a run (you can turn it off). Read the privacy policy.
